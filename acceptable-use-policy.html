Section 01

1. Introduction and Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP", "Policy") is issued by Synerbit (OPC) Private Limited ("Company", "we", "us", "our"), the operator of PulsePM, a unified project management platform accessible at app.pulsepm.in and via our mobile application.

This Policy defines the acceptable and unacceptable ways in which any individual or organisation may access and use the PulsePM platform, its infrastructure, services, data, features, and associated systems (collectively, the "Platform" or "Services").

The purpose of this AUP is to:

  • Protect the security, integrity, availability, and performance of the Platform;
  • Protect the rights, privacy, and safety of all users;
  • Ensure compliance with applicable Indian laws and regulations;
  • Define consequences for misuse and the enforcement process.
Integral Documentation:

This AUP forms an integral part of and must be read alongside:

Terms and Conditions Privacy Policy Community Guidelines

By accessing or using PulsePM in any manner, you agree to be bound by this AUP. If you do not agree, you must immediately cease all use of the Platform.

Section 02

This Policy applies universally across all users, roles, and access mediums:

Platform Mediums Covered:
  • Web Application (app.pulsepm.in)
  • Mobile Application (Android or iOS)
  • APIs or integrations (present or future)
  • Automated notifications, invitations & email alerts

Section 04

4. Permitted Uses

PulsePM is designed exclusively for legitimate, lawful, and professional project and task management activities. The following activities are permitted:

4.1 Core Platform Activities

  • ✓ Creating and managing projects, tasks, subtasks, and project categories
  • ✓ Assigning tasks to team members within a project
  • ✓ Updating task statuses and tracking project progress
  • ✓ Adding comments and feedback on tasks in a professional and constructive manner
  • ✓ Using @mentions to draw a colleague's attention to a specific task or update
  • ✓ Inviting team members, colleagues, clients, or collaborators to join a project workspace
  • ✓ Viewing, editing, and completing assigned tasks within your role's permissions
  • ✓ Organising and prioritising tasks using due dates, priorities, and labels
  • ✓ Syncing project and task data across web and mobile devices for continuity of work
  • ✓ Receiving and managing notifications about task updates and project changes

4.2 Organisational and Business Use

  • ✓ Managing internal team projects and workflows
  • ✓ Tracking client deliverables and project milestones
  • ✓ Coordinating cross-functional or cross-departmental work
  • ✓ Managing freelance or contract work assignments
  • ✓ Onboarding new team members into project workspaces
  • ✓ Archiving completed projects and tasks for record-keeping

4.3 Personal Productivity Use

  • ✓ Managing personal projects, goals, and to-do lists
  • ✓ Tracking personal task progress and deadlines
  • ✓ Self-organising work using PulsePM's task and project structure
Compliance Baseline: All permitted uses must at all times remain consistent with applicable Indian laws, consistent with these Terms, this AUP, and all other Platform policies, and respectful of the rights and privacy of other users.
Section 05

5. Prohibited Uses

The following activities are strictly prohibited on PulsePM. Any user found engaging in these activities will be subject to enforcement action as described in Section 9.

5.1 Illegal Activities

You must not use PulsePM to:

  • Conduct, facilitate, promote, or plan any activity that is unlawful under Indian law or the law of any applicable jurisdiction;
  • Engage in or facilitate fraud, forgery, cheating, financial crime, or money laundering;
  • Plan or coordinate activities that violate the Indian Penal Code, 1860, including criminal conspiracy, extortion, or intimidation;
  • Violate any court order, tribunal direction, or regulatory injunction;
  • Store, transmit, or coordinate the distribution of contraband, drugs, weapons, or other illegal goods or services;
  • Facilitate tax evasion or any violation of the Income Tax Act, 1961, GST laws, or other Indian fiscal regulations.

5.2 Cybersecurity and Technical Violations

You must not:

  • Hack or crack: Attempt unauthorised access to the Platform, servers, databases, network infrastructure, or any user's account — violating Section 43 & Section 66 of the IT Act, 2000;
  • Introduce malware: Upload, transmit, or distribute viruses, worms, ransomware, spyware, trojans, keyloggers, or malicious payloads;
  • Denial of Service: Conduct DoS or DDoS attacks against the Platform or any of its users;
  • Port scan or probe: Probe the Platform's infrastructure to discover vulnerabilities without prior written authorisation from the Company;
  • Exploit bugs: Knowingly exploit any technical flaw in the Platform for personal gain or to harm others (see Section 12 for responsible disclosure);
  • Intercept data: Use packet sniffing, man-in-the-middle attacks, or any other method to intercept communications or data;
  • Bypass security controls: Circumvent authentication mechanisms, access controls, role permissions, session tokens, or rate limiters;
  • Brute-force: Conduct brute-force attacks on user accounts or administrative panels;
  • Inject malicious code: Execute SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), cross-site request forgery (CSRF), or code injection attacks.

5.3 Unauthorised Data Access and Scraping

You must not:

  • Scrape or harvest data from the Platform using automated scripts, bots, crawlers, or automated means;
  • Access, copy, extract, or store any data from other users' projects, tasks, or profiles without explicit authorisation;
  • Use the Platform's API (present or future) in violation of its documented rate limits or usage guidelines;
  • Attempt to reconstruct, reverse-engineer, or clone the Platform's database schema, data structure, or logic;
  • Use any third-party tool or service to extract bulk data from PulsePM for commercial or competitive purposes.

5.4 Account and Identity Violations

You must not:

  • Create multiple accounts for the same individual to evade a ban, restriction, or usage limitation;
  • Impersonate any person, company, government authority, or PulsePM staff member;
  • Use false identity information to create or maintain a PulsePM account;
  • Share your account credentials with another person or allow a third party to access PulsePM using your account;
  • Sell, transfer, or lease your PulsePM account or login credentials to any third party;
  • Use another user's credentials, whether obtained by hacking, phishing, or social engineering — violating Section 66C of the IT Act, 2000 (identity theft).

5.5 Harmful and Offensive Content

You must not create, upload, transmit, or share any content that:

  • Is obscene, pornographic, sexually explicit, or harmful to minors — violating Section 67/67A of the IT Act, 2000 and the POCSO Act, 2012;
  • Is defamatory, libellous, or maliciously false — violating Section 499/500 of the IPC;
  • Constitutes harassment, stalking, or cyberbullying — violating Section 354D of the IPC and Section 67 of the IT Act;
  • Contains threats of violence or incites violence — violating Section 506 of the IPC;
  • Promotes, glorifies, or facilitates terrorism or extremism — violating the UAPA, 1967;
  • Promotes hatred on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, nationality, language, or region — violating Section 153A of the IPC;
  • Spreads disinformation or deliberate misinformation that could cause public harm;
  • Violates the dignity or modesty of women — violating Section 354 of the IPC and Section 67A of the IT Act.

5.6 Privacy Violations

You must not:

  • Collect or store personal data of other users accessed through the Platform for any purpose beyond legitimate project collaboration;
  • Publish or expose another user's private information (contact details, address, ID documents, financial details) without explicit consent ("doxxing");
  • Photograph, screenshot, or record another user's private activity and share it publicly without consent;
  • Process Sensitive Personal Data or Information (SPDI) of other users without explicit, informed consent;
  • Violate any applicable provision of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA).

5.7 Intellectual Property Violations

You must not:

  • Infringe the copyright, trademark, patent, or trade secret of any third party through uploaded content;
  • Upload or share pirated software, cracked applications, or unlicensed digital content;
  • Use the Platform to distribute unauthorised copies of copyrighted material;
  • Copy, reproduce, or reverse-engineer the Platform's source code, design, algorithms, or business logic — violating the Copyright Act, 1957;
  • Use PulsePM's name, logo, or brand in any way that creates a false impression of endorsement, partnership, or affiliation.

5.8 Spam and Unsolicited Communications

You must not:

  • Send bulk, unsolicited, or repetitive messages, notifications, or emails to other users;
  • Use the project invitation system to spam individuals who are not intended collaborators;
  • Use automated tools to mass-create projects, tasks, comments, or invitations;
  • Use PulsePM as a vehicle to market products or services to other users without their consent.

5.9 System Integrity and Performance

You must not:

  • Take any action that overloads, disrupts, damages, or degrades the performance of servers or network infrastructure;
  • Use the Platform in any way that causes a disproportionate burden on our systems;
  • Interfere with security systems, firewalls, or intrusion detection mechanisms;
  • Access the Platform through unauthorised automated bots or scripts that simulate human usage without prior written consent.

5.10 Misuse of Platform Features

You must not:

  • Create fictitious projects, tasks, or activity logs to deceive stakeholders, management, or clients;
  • Manipulate task statuses or completion records to create a misleading picture of project progress;
  • Use the @mention system to repeatedly tag, harass, or spam a specific user;
  • Exploit role-based permission gaps or configuration errors to gain unauthorised access to project data;
  • Store sensitive government identification numbers, financial credentials, or regulated data (such as Aadhaar, PAN details, or banking credentials) in violation of applicable law.
Section 06

6. Authorised Access Only

Access to PulsePM systems and data is granted on a strict need-to-use basis, specific to each user's registered account and role within their projects.

You are authorised to access only those systems, data, and features to which you have been explicitly granted access by your role or workspace administrators.

Statutory Warning: Any attempt to access systems, accounts, or data beyond your authorised scope — whether through technical exploitation or procedural manipulation — constitutes unauthorised access under Section 43 of the IT Act, 2000 and attracts criminal liability under Section 66.

If you discover that you have inadvertently been granted access to data or systems beyond your authorised scope, you must immediately report it to us at contact@pulsepm.in and refrain from using, copying, downloading, or disclosing such data.

Section 07

7. API and Automation Usage

If and when PulsePM makes APIs or programmatic access available:

  • You may only use authorised APIs in accordance with the published API documentation and rate limits;
  • Automated use of the Platform must not exceed the rate limits or usage thresholds specified;
  • You may not use automated means to perform any action on the Platform that is prohibited by this AUP;
  • API access may be revoked at any time if it is found to be in violation of this AUP or the Terms and Conditions;
  • You must not create, use, or distribute any unofficial, unauthorised, or reverse-engineered API for PulsePM.
Section 08

8. Monitoring and Logging

8.1 Right to Monitor

Synerbit (OPC) Private Limited reserves the right to monitor usage of the Platform for security and fraud detection, investigation of reported violations, compliance with this AUP and applicable laws, and platform performance optimisation.

8.2 Log Retention

We maintain server access logs, authentication logs, and usage logs in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable Indian law. These logs may be used as evidence in enforcement actions or legal proceedings.

8.3 No Expectation of Privacy for Violating Activity

While we respect user privacy as described in our Privacy Policy, users have no expectation of privacy with respect to activities that violate this AUP, applicable law, or the rights of others. Such activities and logs may be disclosed to law enforcement authorities or in legal proceedings.

Section 09

9. Enforcement

9.1 Enforcement Principles

We are committed to fair, consistent, and proportional enforcement of this AUP. Enforcement decisions evaluate the nature and severity of the violation, whether it is isolated or repeated, user intent (accidental vs. deliberate), and the potential or actual harm caused.

9.2 Enforcement Actions (5-Tier Framework)

Severity Level Nature of Violation Action Taken
Level 1 — Minor Unintentional or isolated technical misuse; minor policy breach Written warning by email; guidance issued
Level 2 — Moderate Repeated minor violations; deliberate misuse of features Temporary feature restriction or rate limiting
Level 3 — Serious Wilful violation causing limited harm; first-time serious offence Account suspension (7–30 days); content removal
Level 4 — Severe Wilful violation causing significant harm; repeated serious offences Permanent account termination; all data deleted
Level 5 — Criminal Hacking, data theft, CSAM, terrorism, extortion, identity theft Immediate permanent ban + referral to law enforcement
9.3 Immediate Action

We reserve the right to act immediately without notice if an active security threat or criminal violation is detected.

9.4 No Refunds

No refunds will be issued for paid subscription periods when an account is terminated or suspended for AUP violations.

9.5 Law Enforcement

We cooperate fully with Indian law enforcement agencies, cybercrime cells, and judicial courts.

Section 10

10. Reporting a Violation

If you observe or suspect a violation of this AUP by another user or third party, please submit a formal report to our team.

10.1 How to Report

📧 Email: contact@pulsepm.in

Subject Line: AUP Violation Report

Include in your report:
  • Your name and registered email address
  • Description of the suspected violation
  • The username, project name, or task/comment involved
  • Approximate date and time of the incident
  • Any screenshots or supporting evidence

10.2 What Happens After You Report

Stage Timeframe
Acknowledgement of report Within 24 hours
Preliminary assessment Within 3 business days
Investigation Within 15 business days
Outcome communicated (to reporter) Within 20 business days

10.3 Good-Faith Requirement: Reports must be submitted in good faith based on a genuine belief that a violation has occurred. Filing false, frivolous, or malicious reports is itself a violation of this AUP and may result in action against the reporter's account.

Section 11

11. Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action taken against your account was incorrect or based on a misunderstanding, you may submit a formal appeal.

11.1 How to Appeal

📧 Email: contact@pulsepm.in

Subject Line: AUP Enforcement Appeal — [Your Registered Email]

Include a clear explanation of why you believe the enforcement action was erroneous, along with any relevant corroborating evidence.

11.2 Appeal Review Timeline

Stage Timeframe
Acknowledgement Within 3 business days
Review and investigation Within 15 business days
Final decision communicated Within 20 business days

11.3 Finality of Decision: The decision issued upon appeal is final and binding. No further reviews will be conducted for the same incident unless significant new evidence is presented.

Section 12

12. Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure

We are committed to maintaining the security of PulsePM for all users. If you discover a genuine security vulnerability in our Platform, we encourage you to report it responsibly rather than exploit it.

12.1 How to Report a Security Vulnerability

📧 Email: contact@pulsepm.in

Subject Line: Security Vulnerability Report

Include: Description of vulnerability, steps to reproduce, potential impact, and technical logs.

12.2 Our Commitment

  • Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours;
  • Investigate and validate the reported vulnerability;
  • Work to remediate the issue in a reasonable timeframe;
  • Maintain confidentiality about the vulnerability until it is patched.
12.3 What Responsible Disclosure Is NOT:

Responsible disclosure does not authorise you to access, copy, or exfiltrate user data, disrupt services, exploit flaws for personal gain, or publicly disclose bugs before they are remediated.

Section 13

13. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this AUP at any time. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document;
  • Publish the revised AUP at pulsepm.in/acceptable-use-policy;
  • Display an in-platform notice across web and mobile applications;
  • Notify registered users by email at least 15 days before material changes take effect.

Your continued use of PulsePM after the effective date of the revised AUP constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
