Culture

Why Join PulsePM?

Traditional project management tools are glorified to-do lists designed for status meetings. Engineers hate filling them out, engineering managers spend hours chasing updates, and leadership still has no idea when products are actually shipping.

PulsePM is rewriting this paradigm. By analyzing commit velocity, pull request cycles, and blockers algorithmically, we give teams automated, live project cardiograms. Working at PulsePM means you will be solving complex distributed system problems, building low-latency collaborative UI, and making life better for thousands of developers worldwide.

🎯

Craftsmanship
