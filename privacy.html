9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures as required under Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules, 2011 and the DPDPA, 2023, including:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest: Passwords are hashed and salted using Firebase Authentication's industry-standard cryptography. Project and task data is stored securely in Google Firestore with access controlled by Firebase Security Rules.
  • Authentication controls: We support email/password login and Google OAuth. We recommend using strong, unique passwords.
  • Access controls: Only authorised personnel of Synerbit (OPC) Pvt Ltd can access production systems and data.
  • Session management: User sessions are managed securely with automatic timeout mechanisms.
  • Local data security: Data cached in your browser's localStorage is protected by browser-level sandboxing.
Note: No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach that is likely to cause harm to you, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

10.1 Cookies

PulsePM may use session cookies and authentication cookies strictly necessary for the Platform to function. These cookies:

  • Do not track you across third-party websites
  • Are not used for advertising purposes
  • Are deleted when you close your browser (session cookies)

10.2 Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to:

  • Cache your project and task data for faster load times and offline access
  • Store your UI preferences (theme, display settings)

You can clear localStorage data at any time through your browser settings. Note that clearing it will log you out and remove cached data from your device.

10.3 Third-Party Cookies

Google Firebase SDKs loaded by our Platform may set their own cookies as part of authentication and analytics. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

12.1 Right to Access

You have the right to obtain information about what Personal Data we hold about you, the purposes of processing, and entities shared with.

12.2 Right to Correction & Erasure

You have the right to correct inaccurate data and erase data no longer necessary for its original purpose or where consent is withdrawn.

You have the right to have your grievances addressed within a reasonable timeframe (see Section 16).
