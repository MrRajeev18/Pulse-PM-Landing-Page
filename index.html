Why We Built PulsePM: Moving from Stale Trackers to Living Telemetry

Every product leader knows the pain: Monday standups are optimistic, but by Thursday night, unexpected blockers surface, PRs sit unreviewed for 48 hours, and sprint commitments collapse.

We asked ourselves: Why do cardiologists get continuous, millisecond-accurate ECG monitors, while product teams manage mission-critical roadmaps with static sticky notes?

PulsePM changes the game. Instead of forcing engineers to manually drag cards across Kanban columns, PulsePM plugs into your code repositories, CI/CD runners, design prototypes, and communication channels. It measures the genuine pulse of work in progress, calculates predictive health indices, and provides instant checkmark verification for every completed requirement.
